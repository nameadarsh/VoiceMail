  1. Go to your Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com
  2. Enable 2-Step Verification if not already enabled:
    • Go to Security
    • Click on "2-Step Verification"
    • Follow the steps to enable it
  3. Generate an App Password:
    • Go back to Security
    • Click on "App Passwords" (requires 2-Step Verification)
    • Select "Mail" for app and "Other" for device
    • Name it "Voice Email Assistant"
    • Copy the 16-character password generated
  4. Use this App Password in the login form below
Use an App Password from your Google Account, not your regular password.
