Quick Guide

  1. Click the microphone button 🎤 next to any field to start recording
  2. Speak clearly into your microphone
  3. Click the microphone button again to stop recording
  4. The recorded text will appear in the field
  5. You can also type manually in any field

Make sure your browser has permission to use the microphone.
